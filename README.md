# Walmart Queue Watch

A tiny bookmarklet that shows your live spot in a Walmart waiting-room queue — your **position**, an **ETA countdown**, and a **"GO!"** alert the moment it's your turn to buy. No install, no account, nothing to download.

### → Get it: **https://pseudonymous7.github.io/line-watch/**

Open that page, drag the button up to your bookmarks bar, and click it whenever you're stuck in a Walmart drop's "you're in line" waiting room.

It only reads your place in line — the same info the page already shows you. It never touches your password, payment, or cart.
