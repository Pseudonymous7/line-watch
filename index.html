Walmart Queue Watch

See your spot in the Walmart line 🎟️

Stuck in a Walmart drop's "waiting room"? This shows your place in line, how long until your turn, and flashes the tab the second it's your turn to buy. No download, no sign-up — it just reads the same info the page already has.

Step 1. Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar:

📍 Walmart Queue Watch

(Don't see a bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show it — ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.)

  1. Step 2. Go do your Walmart drop like normal. When you hit the "you're in line" waiting room…
  2. Step 3. Click the 📍 Walmart Queue Watch bookmark you just made.
  3. A little box pops up with your number + countdown. When it turns green and says GO! — buy it!
Can't drag it?
Click the bookmark again to close the box. It only works while you're on the Walmart waiting-room page (that's where your spot lives). It never sees your password or payment — it only reads your place in line.
