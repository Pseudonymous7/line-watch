Round1 Pass

Grab your Round1 pass 🎟️

Through the Round1 waiting room? This copies your pass in one click, so every one of your bot tasks can skip the line too — and you can share it with your group. No download, no sign-up: it only reads the pass the page already gave you.

Step 1. Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar:

🎟️ Round1 Pass

(Don't see a bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show it — ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.)

  1. Step 2. Do your Round1 drop as normal. Wait until you're through the line and the entry form loads.
  2. Step 3. Click the 🎟️ Round1 Pass bookmark you just made — it copies your pass to the clipboard.
  3. In the bot, open Settings → Store settings → Round1, paste it into Waiting room pass, and hit Save. Every task now skips the line.
Can't drag it?
Run it on the Round1 registration page once you're through the line — that's where the pass lives, and it lasts about 30 minutes. It never sees your password or payment; it only reads your admission pass. A pass shared with your group lets all of you skip the line.
